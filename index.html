Most people pick a lane: marketing or operations. My last two roles asked for both, so I built both. At Babo Finance I ran the full marketing calendar for an Australian mortgage firm and screened confidential loan files for compliance the same week — same person, same standard of accuracy on either side of the job.

Before that, I led a 26-person team processing medical records for hospitals and clinics nationwide, where the job was tracing why files went missing and rebuilding the pipeline so they stopped going missing. That's the same instinct I bring to a content calendar: find where the process breaks, fix the system, not just the symptom.

I don't need the brief re-explained twice, and I don't wait to be handed the next task. If there's a gap between "what marketing wants" and "what compliance requires," I'm comfortable being the person standing in it.
